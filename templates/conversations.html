Daily Kannada Conversations

Learn how to speak Kannada in everyday situations.

🏫 At College

Person 1: ನಿಮ್ಮ ಹೆಸರು ಏನು?

Meaning: What is your name?

Person 2: ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ಮುಸ್ಕಾನ್.

Meaning: My name is Muskan.

🛍️ At Shopping

Person 1: ಇದು ಎಷ್ಟು?

Meaning: How much is this?

Person 2: ನೂರು ರೂಪಾಯಿ.

Meaning: One hundred rupees.

🚕 At Travel

Person 1: ಬಸ್ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ?

Meaning: Where is the bus stop?

Person 2: ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ.

Meaning: It is there.

🏥 At Hospital

Person 1: ನನಗೆ ಸಹಾಯ ಬೇಕು.

Meaning: I need help.

Person 2: ಸರಿ, ಸಹಾಯ ಮಾಡುತ್ತೇನೆ.

Meaning: Okay, I will help.
